## What are your opening hours?
We are open Monday to Saturday, 8am to 6pm (WAT). We are closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## Where are you located?
Our office is at 12 Aminu Kano Crescent, Wuse II, Abuja. We also deliver nationwide.

## How long does delivery take?
Deliveries within Abuja and Lagos take 1 to 2 working days. Other states take 3 to 5 working days.

## How much is delivery?
Delivery is free for orders above ₦50,000. Otherwise it costs ₦2,500 within Abuja and Lagos and ₦4,000 elsewhere.

## What payment methods do you accept?
We accept card payments and bank transfers through Paystack, USSD, and pay on delivery in Abuja and Lagos.

## Can I return a product?
Yes. You can return unused items within 7 days of delivery for a full refund. Contact support with your order number.

## How do I track my order?
Use the tracking link in your confirmation email or SMS, or send us your order number and we will check it for you.

## How do I contact support?
Email support@example.com, call or WhatsApp 0800 000 0000, Monday to Saturday, 8am to 6pm.

## Do you offer discounts for bulk orders?
Yes. Orders of 20 units or more get 10 percent off. Contact sales for a custom quote.

## Is my payment information safe?
Yes. Payments are processed by Paystack, which is PCI DSS compliant. We never store your card details.
